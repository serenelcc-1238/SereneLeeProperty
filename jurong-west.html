Jurong West property: common questions

How much is a 4-room HDB flat in Jurong West?

In the last 12 months (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 573 4-room flats resold in Jurong West at a median price of $535,000. Prices depend mostly on the remaining lease, floor and distance to the MRT.

Which MRT stations serve Jurong West?

Most Jurong West HDB blocks are nearest to Lakeside MRT, Pioneer MRT or Boon Lay MRT station, based on OneMap locations.

Is Jurong West a good place to upgrade or right-size?

Jurong West has a range of flat ages and sizes, with 25 condo and EC projects in and around the estate, so upgraders and right-sizers can often stay in the same area. The right choice depends on your budget, CPF and timeline; Serene can work out the numbers with you.

Yes. Lucerne Grand, Sora, J'Den are in D22, the postal district around Jurong West. See the New Launches page for details.
