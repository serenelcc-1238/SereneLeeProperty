November 2026 BTO Launch: 7 Projects, 7,964 Flats — What Buyers Need to Know

Updated 12 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

HDB’s final BTO exercise of 2026 opened on 4 Nov with 7,964 flats across 7 projects in 6 towns. It is also the first launch open to households earning up to $16,000 a month.

At a glance

  • When (official): applications closed 11 Nov 2026 (11.59pm). Computer ballot, not first-come-first-served.
  • Demand (official HDB data, as at 5pm 11 Nov): 35,603 applicants for 7,964 flats — 4.5 per flat, the highest BTO application rate since Aug 2022.
  • How many (official): 7,964 flats in Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun.
  • Where: more than half the flats sit in mature estates — a rare chance to buy subsidised in towns where resale carries a premium.
  • Tiers (official): 3 Standard, 3 Plus, 1 Prime. Plus and Prime flats carry a 10-year MOP and a subsidy recovery of 8–11% when you sell.
  • Cheapest entry (official): 2-room Flexi at Plantation Dew from $131,000 before grants.
  • Waiting times (official): Sembawang Anchor 2 yrs 10 mths (completion around 2029); Bayshore Oasis and Plantation Dew 3 yrs 2 mths; Merpati Meadow 3 yrs 3 mths; Bayshore Sails 3 yrs 8 mths; Toa Payoh Hillside 3 yrs 9 mths. All under 4 years.
  • Year total: rounds off ~19,600 BTO flats for 2026 across the February, June and November exercises.
  • Hot picks: Toa Payoh Hillside and Plantation Dew (Tengah) drew the most demand. Toa Payoh Hillside 4-room: more than 6 first-timer applicants per flat. Plantation Dew 4-room: more than 5.

What changed since the preview

  • Higher income ceilings (effective 24 Aug 2026, announced at NDR 2026): families $14,000 → $16,000; singles aged 35+ $7,000 → $8,000.
  • Launch moved to November so newly eligible households could secure an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter in time.
  • HFE document deadline was 25 Sep 2026. If you did not submit by then, you cannot ballot in this exercise — see Plan B below.
  • Extra ballot chance per child for first-timer families does not apply yet. It starts from the February 2027 exercise.

Project quick takes

Bayshore Sails & Bayshore Oasis (Bedok) — 2,492 flats, the biggest share

  • Beside Bayshore MRT on the Thomson-East Coast Line, a short walk to East Coast Park.
  • Bayshore Oasis (official): 855 flats (2-room Flexi and 4-room) in 4 blocks, with a shorter waiting time of 3 years 2 months.
  • Bayshore Sails (official): 1,637 flats (2-room Flexi, 3-room and 4-room), waiting time 3 years 8 months.
  • Plus (official): 10-year MOP and 9% subsidy recovery for both projects.
  • Over half the supply is 2-room Flexi — strong for singles and seniors.
  • Application rate (5pm 11 Nov): 4-room drew 3.1 first-timer applicants per flat.
  • Value check: Vela Bay, the area’s first condo, sold 72% of units on launch weekend at an average of ~$2,886 psf.
  • Compare with resale prices nearby in the Bedok town guide.

Toa Payoh Hillside (Toa Payoh) — the hottest 4-room ticket

  • Right next to the Caldecott CCL/TEL interchange, in a mature estate.
  • Prime (official): 10-year MOP and 11% subsidy recovery. Waiting time 3 years 9 months.
  • Only 2-room Flexi and 4-room; no 5-room.
  • Toa Payoh’s first Community Care Apartments (256 units).
  • Application rates (5pm 11 Nov): more than 6 first-timer applicants per 4-room flat. The 256 CCAs had an application rate of 2.
  • Compare with resale prices nearby in the Toa Payoh town guide.

Merpati Meadow (Geylang) — smallest, but competitive

  • Only 438 units, walking distance to Mattar MRT (Downtown Line).
  • Plus (official): 10-year MOP and 8% subsidy recovery. Waiting time 3 years 3 months.
  • Geylang’s last BTO was Merpati Alcove (Oct 2024, 443 units), so pent-up demand is likely.
  • Compare with resale prices nearby in the Geylang town guide.

Chencharu Acres (Yishun) — best for larger families

  • Largest 5-room supply in this launch: 644 units (official).
  • Fifth project in the Chencharu precinct near Khatib MRT; 2 preschools planned on-site.
  • Compare with resale prices nearby, including blocks near Khatib MRT, in the Yishun town guide.

Sembawang Anchor (Sembawang North) — space at entry-level prices

  • Shorter waiting time (official): 2 years 10 months, completion around 2029. Between Admiralty Lane and Sembawang Drive, with a preschool, supermarket and shops on-site.
  • More than two-thirds of the 1,310 flats are 4-room or 5-room.
  • Sembawang already saw 3,950 flats launched in February and June. Application rates (5pm 11 Nov): undersubscribed among first-timer families for 4-room and 5-room (below 1).
  • Compare with resale prices nearby in the Sembawang town guide.

Plantation Dew (Tengah) — first Tengah launch in ~3 years

  • Shorter waiting time (official): 3 years 2 months.
  • White Flats: open-concept units with no internal partition walls — the first BTO to offer them in 5-room flats. They cost $6,000 (3-room), $8,600 (4-room) and $9,000 (5-room) less than standard layouts.
  • Walking distance to upcoming Jurong Region Line stations (opening in stages from 2028 to 2029).
  • Plantation district; every flat type on offer, including the launch’s only 28 3Gen flats.
  • Prices (official): 2-room Flexi from $131,000 and 4-room from $339,000 before grants — among the cheapest entry points this round.
  • Most contested flat type: 2-room Flexi drew 1,619 applicants for 178 flats — more than 18 times oversubscribed among singles (5pm 11 Nov).

My ballot strategy tips

  • Pick by need, not hype. Toa Payoh Hillside and Bayshore will be oversubscribed; a project you can actually win beats one you only dream about.
  • Price in the 10-year MOP. Plus and Prime flats lock you in for a decade, and HDB claws back part of the subsidy when you sell. If you plan to upgrade within 5–7 years, a Standard flat fits better.
  • Ballot results: if you applied for Sembawang Anchor 4- or 5-room as a first-timer family, your odds are very good. Toa Payoh and Tengah applicants should line up Plan B now.
  • Budget beyond the price tag. Add renovation and several years of waiting time (and rent, if you need a home now).
  • Have a Plan B ready before results come out.

Missed the HFE deadline — or the ballot? Your Plan B

  • Next BTO: February 2027. About 4,300 flats in Bishan, Tampines, Tengah and Toa Payoh, plus a Sale of Balance Flats exercise (~5,000 flats). It is the first exercise with an extra ballot chance per child for first-timer families. Submit your HFE application by 15 Dec 2026.
  • HDB resale: no multi-year wait, you choose the exact unit and floor, and CPF Housing Grants still apply.
  • Upgrading instead? Bayshore’s condo launch shows how wide the BTO-to-private price gap is — worth running the numbers before you commit to a 10-year MOP.

Key dates

  • 24 Aug 2026: new income ceilings take effect.
  • 25 Sep 2026: HFE supporting documents due for the November exercise (passed).
  • 4–11 Nov 2026: BTO applications open (closed 11.59pm on 11 Nov).
  • 15 Dec 2026: submit HFE application to be in time for February 2027.
  • February 2027: next BTO exercise.
  • Mar–Jun 2027: flat selection appointments for this exercise at HDB Hub, Toa Payoh.
