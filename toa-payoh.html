Toa Payoh property: common questions

How much is a 4-room HDB flat in Toa Payoh?

In the last 12 months (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 409 4-room flats resold in Toa Payoh at a median price of $1,000,000. Prices depend mostly on the remaining lease, floor and distance to the MRT.

Which MRT stations serve Toa Payoh?

Most Toa Payoh HDB blocks are nearest to Toa Payoh MRT, Braddell MRT, Potong Pasir MRT, Woodleigh MRT, Bartley MRT, Caldecott MRT or Mattar MRT station, based on OneMap locations.

Is Toa Payoh a good place to upgrade or right-size?

Toa Payoh has a range of flat ages and sizes, with 79 condo and EC projects in and around the estate, so upgraders and right-sizers can often stay in the same area. The right choice depends on your budget, CPF and timeline; Serene can work out the numbers with you.

Are there new launches near Toa Payoh?

Yes. The Orie, The Arcady At Boon Keng are in D13 / D12, the postal districts around Toa Payoh. See the New Launches page for details.
