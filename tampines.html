Tampines property: common questions

How much is a 4-room HDB flat in Tampines?

In the last 12 months (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 876 4-room flats resold in Tampines at a median price of $665,500. Prices depend mostly on the remaining lease, floor and distance to the MRT.

Which MRT stations serve Tampines?

Most Tampines HDB blocks are nearest to Tampines East MRT, Tampines MRT, Tampines West MRT, Simei MRT, Upper Changi MRT or Pasir Ris East MRT station, based on OneMap locations.

Is Tampines a good place to upgrade or right-size?

Tampines has a range of flat ages and sizes, with 52 condo and EC projects in and around the estate, so upgraders and right-sizers can often stay in the same area. The right choice depends on your budget, CPF and timeline; Serene can work out the numbers with you.

Are there new launches near Tampines?
