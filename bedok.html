Bedok property: common questions

How much is a 4-room HDB flat in Bedok?

In the last 12 months (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 527 4-room flats resold in Bedok at a median price of $600,000. Prices depend mostly on the remaining lease, floor and distance to the MRT.

Which MRT stations serve Bedok?

Most Bedok HDB blocks are nearest to Bedok MRT, Bedok Reservoir MRT, Kaki Bukit MRT, Bedok North MRT, Tanah Merah MRT, Bedok South MRT, Kembangan MRT or Bayshore MRT station, based on OneMap locations.

Is Bedok a good place to upgrade or right-size?

Bedok has a range of flat ages and sizes, with 106 condo and EC projects in and around the estate, so upgraders and right-sizers can often stay in the same area. The right choice depends on your budget, CPF and timeline; Serene can work out the numbers with you.

Are there new launches near Bedok?

Yes. Vela Bay, Pinery Residences, Ocho, Bagnall Haus are in D14 / D16, the postal districts around Bedok. See the New Launches page for details.
