Singapore Property Updates: September 2026

Hi there,

Here's what happened in Singapore's HDB resale market in September 2026, straight from HDB's official data, plus a closer look at Woodlands.

3 things to know

  • 2,243 HDB flats were resold across Singapore in September 2026, up 3% on September 2025. The busiest towns were Tampines (189), Sengkang (166) and Punggol (150).
  • 208 flats sold for $1 million or more (vs 172 a year earlier). Top sale: a 5-room flat at Blk 1C Cantonment Rd, Central Area (floor 19-21, 107 sqm) for $1,720,000.
  • Town in focus, Woodlands: 147 resales; 4-room median $535k, down 6.1% on a year ago (73 deals vs 68). Medians mix flats of different ages, so check your own block.
