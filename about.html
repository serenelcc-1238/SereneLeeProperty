About Serene

Real numbers, before real estate talk

I still remember how overwhelming it felt trying to make sense of my own first big property decision — the numbers, the timing, the "what ifs." That's exactly why I approach every client relationship the way I do.

I believe a home isn't just a transaction, it's a chapter in your life. So before I ever show you a listing, I want to sit down and really understand yours — your budget, your timeline, your dreams for where you're headed next. My approach is always numbers-first: clear, honest figures, not guesswork or sales pressure.

Because a house isn't just where you'll live — it's part of a much longer story. I want to be the person who walks every chapter of that story with you, not just this one.

Here's my promise to you:

  • Commitment — I'm by your side through every milestone, from your very first home to your long-term plans.
  • Authenticity — You'll always get my honest take, even when it's not what you hoped to hear.
  • Reliability — Steady and dependable, through every twist the market throws at us.
  • Empowerment — I'll make sure you always feel clear and confident, never pressured.

Buying, selling, or renting — HDB or private, anywhere in Singapore — I'd love to be part of your journey.
