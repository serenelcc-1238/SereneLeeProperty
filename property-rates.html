Property rates & rules

Singapore property rates and rules, on one page

Loan limits, stamp duties and the new launch payment schedule that every buyer and seller should know.

Every rate checked against IRAS, MAS and HDB on 10 Nov 2026. Compiled with AI assistance and reviewed by Serene Lee. Rates can change with new cooling measures, so check the official source before you commit.

LTV

Loan-to-value limits

How much a bank can lend, as a share of the price or valuation (whichever is lower).

All buyersAll bank housing loans
Outstanding housing loansLTV limitLTV if tenure > 30 yrs (HDB flat > 25 yrs) or loan runs past age 65Min. cash down payment

  • Maximum loan tenure: 30 years for HDB flats, 35 years for private property.
  • Joint borrowers: age is the income-weighted average age (each borrower's age × their share of combined income).
  • HDB housing loan: LTV 75% (down from 80% since 20 Aug 2024).
  • Borrowers that are companies or other non-individuals: LTV 15%.

Source: MAS, loan tenure and LTV limits · HDB, 20 Aug 2024 HDB loan LTV

TDSR & MSR

Debt servicing limits

Caps on how much of your income can go to loan repayments.

All buyersBank loans and HDB loans
RatioLimitApplies to
TDSR (Total Debt Servicing Ratio)55% of gross monthly incomeAll property loans from banks: covers all monthly debts (home, car, personal, student loans, credit cards)
MSR (Mortgage Servicing Ratio)30% of gross monthly incomeHDB flats and ECs: covers all property loan repayments
  • Applies when the OTP was granted on or after 16 Dec 2021.
  • Banks assess TDSR at a stress-test interest rate, not today's rate, and count only part of variable income (bonus, commission, rental). Ask your banker for your exact figure.

Source: MAS, MSR and TDSR rules · MAS, TDSR thresholds

ABSD

Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty

Paid on top of BSD, based on who is buying and how many homes they own. Rates from 27 Apr 2023.

Selected buyersCash, CPF reimbursement possibleDue within 14 days of exercising the OTP / signing
Buyer1st home2nd home3rd and later
Singapore Citizen0%20%30%
Singapore PR5%30%35%
Foreigner60%60%60%
Entity / trustee65%65%65%
  • Joint buyers pay the highest rate that applies to any of them.
  • Free trade agreements: nationals of the USA, and nationals and PRs of Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland, pay the same as Singapore Citizens.
  • Married couples (at least one Singapore Citizen), buying jointly: ABSD on a 1st home is remitted. On a 2nd home, the couple can claim a refund if they sell their 1st home within 6 months of buying (for an uncompleted property, within 6 months of TOP or CSC, whichever is earlier) and meet IRAS conditions.
  • Housing developers: 35% plus a non-remittable 5%.

Source: IRAS, ABSD · IRAS, ABSD under FTAs · IRAS, ABSD remission for married couples
