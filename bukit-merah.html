Bukit Merah property: common questions

How much is a 4-room HDB flat in Bukit Merah?

In the last 12 months (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 385 4-room flats resold in Bukit Merah at a median price of $935,000. Prices depend mostly on the remaining lease, floor and distance to the MRT.

Which MRT stations serve Bukit Merah?

Most Bukit Merah HDB blocks are nearest to Tiong Bahru MRT, Redhill MRT, Telok Blangah MRT, Havelock MRT, Keppel MRT, Cantonment MRT, Queenstown MRT, Harbourfront MRT, Labrador Park MRT or Outram Park MRT station, based on OneMap locations.

Is Bukit Merah a good place to upgrade or right-size?

Bukit Merah has a range of flat ages and sizes, with 118 condo and EC projects in and around the estate, so upgraders and right-sizers can often stay in the same area. The right choice depends on your budget, CPF and timeline; Serene can work out the numbers with you.

Are there new launches near Bukit Merah?

Yes. Amberwood At Holland, Zyon Grand, Penrith, Promenade Peak, Upperhouse At Orchard Boulevard, The River Opus are in D10 / D03, the postal districts around Bukit Merah. See the New Launches page for details.
