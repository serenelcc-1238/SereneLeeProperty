The Serra Residences: VIP Preview Confirmed for 2–13 October

By Serene Lee, ERA Realty · Published 6 Sep 2026 · Updated 6 Sep 2026 · 1 min read

A new freehold project in Novena has just confirmed its preview dates — freehold sites this size in District 11 don't come up often.

It's confirmed: VIP preview 2–13 October 2026, target booking 16/17 October. As an ERA Realty salesperson, I can register you for the VIP preview.

Why This One's Worth a Closer Look

  • Freehold, not leasehold — rare at this scale in D9/D10/D11; most recent supply has been 99-year leasehold GLS sites
  • No lease-decay risk — never hits the CPF/financing restrictions that kick in once a 99-year lease crosses 60 years left (see my lease decay guide)
  • Easier long-term resale — freehold typically keeps a wider buyer pool decades from now

MRT, Schools and Medical

  • MRT: Walking distance to Novena and Newton MRT (North-South / Downtown Line interchange)
  • Schools: Within the Novena / Bukit Timah school belt — matters for upgrading families thinking years ahead
  • Medical: Near Tan Tock Seng Hospital, Mount Elizabeth Novena Hospital and specialist centres

Who Should Actually Consider This

  • Buyers who want freehold tenure for long-term holding or legacy planning, and are willing to pay for it
  • Upgraders choosing the Novena / Newton area itself, not a specific new precinct
  • Investors wanting a smaller, defensive freehold allocation alongside larger leasehold holdings

Not you? A leasehold launch elsewhere may cost less per sq ft — compare on my New Launches page.
