The Serra Residences: VIP Preview Confirmed for 2–13 October

By Serene Lee, ERA Realty · Published 6 Sep 2026 · Updated 6 Sep 2026 · 6 min read

A new freehold project in Novena has just confirmed its preview dates, and it's worth a closer look — not because it's the biggest launch of the year, but because freehold sites this size in District 11 don't come up often.

It's confirmed: VIP preview runs 2–13 October 2026, with target booking on 16/17 October. As an ERA Realty salesperson, I can register interested buyers for the VIP preview and walk you through the unit mix and floor plans once they're released. What follows is a summary of what's publicly confirmed so far, put together to help you decide whether it's worth a closer look — and if it is, how to secure a preview slot.

The Basics

  • Project name: The Serra Residences
  • Developer: Far East Organization, via Transurban Properties Pte Ltd
  • Address: 7 Bassein Road
  • District: D11 (Novena / Newton / Bukit Timah fringe)
  • Tenure: Freehold
  • Site area: Approximately 51,395 sq ft
  • Scale: 133 units across a single 28-storey block
  • Parking: 106 car park lots plus 2 accessible lots
  • Architect: P&T Consultants, with interior design by 932 Design Group and landscape design by Ecoplan Asia

Freehold launches of any real scale in the D9/D10/D11 core are increasingly rare — most new supply in the last few years has come from 99-year leasehold Government Land Sales sites. That scarcity is usually the first thing worth understanding about a project like this, before getting into unit mix or pricing.

Unit Mix

The Serra Residences is positioned as a boutique, low-density development rather than a mass-market one. Based on publicly available information, the mix leans toward smaller, more efficient layouts alongside a handful of larger units — typical of a 133-unit freehold site where land cost per unit is high. If unit mix and exact layouts are something you're weighing against other projects, I'd recommend confirming the finalised e-brochure once it's released, since mixes are sometimes adjusted between preview and launch.

Location: What's Actually Around It

Novena has quietly become one of the better-connected addresses in the city fringe, and Bassein Road sits within that footprint:

  • MRT access: Within walking distance of Novena MRT (North-South Line) and Newton MRT (North-South Line / Downtown Line interchange)
  • Medical hub: Close to Novena's medical cluster — Tan Tock Seng Hospital, Mount Elizabeth Novena Hospital and several specialist centres — a factor some buyers weigh heavily for long-term convenience
  • Retail: Near United Square, Square 2, and Velocity@Novena Square
  • Schools: Within the broader Novena / Bukit Timah school belt, an area many upgraders specifically look for
  • City access: A short drive or MRT ride to Orchard Road and the CBD

None of this is unique to this one project — several other D11 developments share the same strengths. But it's a reasonable starting point for comparing this site against other city-fringe options you might already be considering.

Timeline

  • VIP preview: 2 – 13 October 2026
  • Target booking: 16 / 17 October 2026
  • TOP: Not yet confirmed — reported estimates vary by source, so I'd treat any specific TOP quarter as provisional until the developer states it officially

Preview and booking dates can occasionally shift by a few days as a project gets closer to launch, so it's worth confirming your slot with me directly rather than relying on this page alone if you're planning around a specific date.

On Pricing

Pricing has not been officially released at the time of writing, so I won't speculate on a specific PSF figure here — anything you see quoted before an official price list is out should be treated as an estimate, not a commitment. What's been discussed in industry commentary is the general expectation that a freehold, low-density project in this location would likely command a premium over comparable 99-year leasehold launches nearby, given land scarcity and tenure alone. That's a reasonable expectation to hold, but it's not the same as a confirmed number, and I'd rather tell you that plainly than guess at a PSF that could be wrong by launch day.

Who Might This Suit?

Based purely on the profile of the project — freehold, boutique, city-fringe, medical-hub-adjacent — this tends to appeal to a few different buyer types:

  • Buyers who specifically want freehold tenure for long-term holding or legacy planning, and are willing to pay for it
  • Upgraders drawn to the Novena / Newton area for its schools, medical facilities, and connectivity, rather than a specific new precinct
  • Investors looking at boutique freehold developments as a smaller, more defensive allocation compared to large leasehold mega-projects

Whether any of that actually applies to you depends on your own numbers and priorities — which is really the point of writing this up as a spotlight rather than a sales pitch.
