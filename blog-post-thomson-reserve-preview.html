Thomson Reserve: Prices, Unit Mix and Key Facts

By Serene Lee, ERA Realty · Published 12 Oct 2026 · Updated 17 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

1,268 new homes next to Upper Thomson MRT, on the former Thomson View site. Official prices are out: from $2,608 psf, with 2-bedrooms from $1.544M. The preview is on now, and sales launch on 31 October 2026.

Update, 17 Oct: this post now uses the developer's official news release, including prices, the full unit mix and the TOP date.

Key Facts

  • Developer: Tamarind Development Pte Ltd, a joint venture of CapitaLand Development, UOL Group and Singapore Land Group
  • Location: Bright Hill Drive, District 20 (Upper Thomson / Bishan)
  • Size: 1,268 units in 6 towers: four 21-storey towers (Classic Collection, 740 units) and two 30-storey towers (Luxury Collection, 528 units)
  • Units: 2 to 5 bedrooms, 592 to 1,808 sq ft; 112 homes have a private lift
  • Tenure: 99-year leasehold from 14 July 2026
  • Expected TOP: 2H 2030; estimated vacant possession by 28 February 2031
  • Sales launch: 31 October 2026
  • Sales gallery: Upper Serangoon Crescent (opposite Riversails), not at the site. Open daily, 10am to 7pm.

Why It's Worth a Look

  • MRT at the doorstep: a sheltered walkway links it to Upper Thomson MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line)
  • Connections: one stop to Bright Hill (a future Cross Island Line interchange), one stop to Caldecott (Circle Line), two stops to Stevens (Downtown Line) and five stops to Orchard
  • School: Ai Tong School within 1 km
  • Nature: Windsor Nature Park within a five-minute walk; MacRitchie and Lower Peirce reservoirs a few minutes' drive away
  • Space: the towers take up 12% of the site, with the rest given to landscaping, water features and facilities, including eight pools

What to Weigh Up

  • Mostly 2-bedroom: 716 of the 1,268 units (56%) are 2-bedrooms, so expect plenty of the same unit type on the resale market later
  • Fewer 3-bedroom units: 350 in total — families who want one should look early
  • New launch premium: compare the price list with nearby resale before you commit
