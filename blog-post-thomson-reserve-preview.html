Thomson Reserve: Preview from 17 October

By Serene Lee, ERA Realty · Published 12 Oct 2026 · 2 min read

1,268 new homes next to Upper Thomson MRT, on the former Thomson View site. The preview runs 17–27 October.

Register early: early registrants get priority showflat slots on 17 and 18 October. As an ERA Realty salesperson, I can register you for the preview.

Key Facts

  • Developer: UOL and CapitaLand (50:50 joint venture)
  • Location: Bright Hill Drive, District 20 (Upper Thomson / Bishan)
  • Size: 1,268 units in 6 towers
  • Units: 2 to 5 bedrooms, 592 to 1,808 sq ft
  • Tenure: 99-year leasehold
  • Expected TOP: around Q1 2031 (per ERA's listing)

Why It's Worth a Look

  • MRT at the doorstep: next to Upper Thomson MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line)
  • School: Ai Tong School within 1 km
  • Nature: close to MacRitchie Reservoir, Windsor Nature Park and the Peirce reservoirs
  • Choice: a large site means more stacks, facings and floor levels to pick from

What to Weigh Up

  • Mostly 2-bedroom: about 56% of units are 2-bedroom, so expect more of the same unit type on the resale market later
  • Fewer 3-bedroom units: about 350 in total — families should aim for the first preview weekend
  • New launch premium: compare the price list with nearby resale before you commit

Nearby resale prices, last 12 months (median, from URA caveats):

  • Jadescape: $2,361 psf
  • Thomson Impressions: $2,136 psf
  • Thomson Grand: $1,819 psf

See more transactions on my Market Map.

Who Should Consider This

  • HDB upgraders in Bishan, Ang Mo Kio and Toa Payoh who want to stay in the area
  • Families planning for Ai Tong School
  • Buyers who want a home right next to an MRT station
