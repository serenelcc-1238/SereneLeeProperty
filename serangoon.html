Serangoon property: common questions

How much is a 4-room HDB flat in Serangoon?

In the last 12 months (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 174 4-room flats resold in Serangoon at a median price of $655,194. Prices depend mostly on the remaining lease, floor and distance to the MRT.

Which MRT stations serve Serangoon?

Most Serangoon HDB blocks are nearest to Serangoon MRT or Lorong Chuan MRT station, based on OneMap locations.

Is Serangoon a good place to upgrade or right-size?

Serangoon has a range of flat ages and sizes, with 145 condo and EC projects in and around the estate, so upgraders and right-sizers can often stay in the same area. The right choice depends on your budget, CPF and timeline; Serene can work out the numbers with you.
