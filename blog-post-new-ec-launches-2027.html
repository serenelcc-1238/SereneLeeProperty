Head to head

Where a new EC comes out ahead

The advantages — and the trade-off to weigh against each.

vsBTO
  • Full condo facilities, security and strata title
  • Becomes private property after 10 years
  • Open to second-timers and HDB upgraders
Trade-offBTO is far cheaper and comes with bigger grants.
vsResale HDB
  • Brand-new unit with a fresh 99-year lease
  • No renovation of an ageing flat
  • New EC prices rose 120% from 2015 to 2025, vs 51% for HDB resale
Trade-offResale lets you move in now, with grants of up to $230k.
vsPrivate Condo
  • About 19% lower price psf than new OCR condos
  • CPF Housing Grant of up to $30,000
  • Stay in your HDB while it’s built and pay most at key collection (DPS)
Trade-offA condo has no MOP, no income ceiling and no buyer limits on resale.

Past price growth does not guarantee future returns. DPS typically adds a 2–3% price premium.

Is it for you?

Who should buy one of these 5 ECs

You’re a strong fit if one of these sounds like you.

👪

First-timer families earning up to $12,000

Get the CPF Housing Grant for ECs (up to $30,000) plus condo living below new-launch prices.

📈

Families earning $12,001–$16,000

Too much for most HDB grants, still eligible here. Your most affordable route into a new condo.

🏠

HDB upgraders past MOP

Use DPS to stay in your flat during construction and sell after key collection. These are the last ECs with DPS.

🕑

Buyers staying 5+ years

The 5-year MOP suits owner-occupiers. Fully private at year 10, not 15 as under the new rules.

🚧

Buyers who can wait 3–4 years

These projects are launching now, so completion is a few years away.

📍

Families in the north & north-west

New homes in Woodlands, Sembawang, Yishun and Bukit Panjang, near family, schools and the upcoming RTS Link.
