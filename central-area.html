Central Area property: common questions

How much is a 4-room HDB flat in Central Area?

In the last 12 months (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 79 4-room flats resold in Central Area at a median price of $870,000. Prices depend mostly on the remaining lease, floor and distance to the MRT.

Which MRT stations serve Central Area?

Most Central Area HDB blocks are nearest to Jalan Besar MRT, Chinatown MRT, Tanjong Pagar MRT or Little India MRT station, based on OneMap locations.

Is Central Area a good place to upgrade or right-size?

Central Area has a range of flat ages and sizes, with 105 condo and EC projects in and around the estate, so upgraders and right-sizers can often stay in the same area. The right choice depends on your budget, CPF and timeline; Serene can work out the numbers with you.

Are there new launches near Central Area?

Yes. Duet @ Emily, River Modern, River Green, The Robertson Opus, The Collective At One Sophia, One Leonie Residences are in D09, the postal district around Central Area. See the New Launches page for details and VIP previews.
