Bishan property: common questions

How much is a 4-room HDB flat in Bishan?

In the last 12 months (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 194 4-room flats resold in Bishan at a median price of $788,000. Prices depend mostly on the remaining lease, floor and distance to the MRT.

Which MRT stations serve Bishan?

Most Bishan HDB blocks are nearest to Bishan MRT, Teck Ghee MRT, Upper Thomson MRT, Marymount MRT, Bright Hill MRT or Braddell MRT station, based on OneMap locations.

Is Bishan a good place to upgrade or right-size?

Bishan has a range of flat ages and sizes, with 57 condo and EC projects in and around the estate, so upgraders and right-sizers can often stay in the same area. The right choice depends on your budget, CPF and timeline; Serene can work out the numbers with you.
