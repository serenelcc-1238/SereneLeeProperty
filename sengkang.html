Sengkang property: common questions

How much is a 4-room HDB flat in Sengkang?

In the last 12 months (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 852 4-room flats resold in Sengkang at a median price of $640,000. Prices depend mostly on the remaining lease, floor and distance to the MRT.

Which MRT stations serve Sengkang?

Most Sengkang HDB blocks are nearest to Fernvale LRT, Ranggung LRT, Compassvale LRT, Bakau LRT, Rumbia LRT, Farmway LRT, Kangkar LRT, Tongkang LRT, Renjong LRT, Sengkang LRT, Buangkok MRT, Cheng Lim LRT, Layar LRT, Sengkang MRT, Kupang LRT or Thanggam LRT station, based on OneMap locations.

Is Sengkang a good place to upgrade or right-size?

Sengkang has a range of flat ages and sizes, with 50 condo and EC projects in and around the estate, so upgraders and right-sizers can often stay in the same area. The right choice depends on your budget, CPF and timeline; Serene can work out the numbers with you.
