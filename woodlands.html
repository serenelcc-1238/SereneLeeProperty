Woodlands property: common questions

How much is a 4-room HDB flat in Woodlands?

In the last 12 months (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 874 4-room flats resold in Woodlands at a median price of $550,000. Prices depend mostly on the remaining lease, floor and distance to the MRT.

Which MRT stations serve Woodlands?

Most Woodlands HDB blocks are nearest to Admiralty MRT, Woodlands MRT, Marsiling MRT, Woodlands South MRT or Woodlands North MRT station, based on OneMap locations.

Is Woodlands a good place to upgrade or right-size?

Woodlands has a range of flat ages and sizes, with 18 condo and EC projects in and around the estate, so upgraders and right-sizers can often stay in the same area. The right choice depends on your budget, CPF and timeline; Serene can work out the numbers with you.
